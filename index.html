ABOUT THE JOURNAL

Connecting AI research with real-world impact.

AI Direct is designed as a scholarly publication for researchers, academics, practitioners, and innovators working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and data-driven technologies.

The journal encourages rigorous research as well as practical applications, with particular attention to challenges and opportunities in Africa and emerging economies.
